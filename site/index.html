I am an undergraduate at Nanjing University, studying Intelligent Software Engineering.

I build interactive systems that help people diagnose model failures and guide how AI agents learn reusable skills.

EmailCV (draft)ScholarGitHubLinkedIn

My interests span human–AI interaction, visual analytics, and reliable AI, with two connected research questions:

  • Where and why do models fail? I build interactive tools that turn practitioners’ concerns into targeted evaluations, reveal underperforming data slices, and support evidence-based repair planning.
  • How can agents learn reusable skills from experience? I explore how execution feedback, behavioral validation, and human review can help agents refine their skill libraries and transfer skills to new tasks.

Methodologically, I combine visual analytics, mixed-initiative interfaces, and behavioral evaluation.

I work with Prof. Ke Xu at iMATE Lab in Nanjing University’s School of Intelligence Science and Technology. I also collaborate with Prof. Weikai Yang at HKUST (Guangzhou), in the Data Science and Analytics and Computational Media and Arts thrusts of the Information Hub, and with Dr. Wenchao Li.

I’m seeking research internships, industry internships, and PhD opportunities. If our research interests align, I’d love to hear from you.
